Rising Phoenix Mural

The phoenix, a symbol of renewal and perseverance, is a reminder to keep moving forward despite hard times. This piece is located at the Georgie Duke Center in downtown Vacaville. The mural was originally at the Vacaville Art Gallery’s “Annex” building, where it was unfortunately defaced by a vandal. In the spirit of the phoenix, that end became only the beginning—and it is back to soaring in its new home. I would like to thank the City of Vacaville, Vacaville Parks and Recreation, Vacaville Art Gallery, Visit Vacaville, and the local community. This piece would not be possible without you.
